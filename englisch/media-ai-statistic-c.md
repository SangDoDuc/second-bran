Daten: "If artificial intelligence programs could do each of the following, I'd be …" (in % of US adults, 2021)

| Task | very/somewhat excited | equally | very/somewhat concerned |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perform household chores | 57 | 24 | 19 |
| Perform repetitive workplace tasks | 46 | 27 | 26 |
| Diagnose medical problems | 40 | 24 | 35 |
| Handle customer service calls | 27 | 26 | 47 |
| Make important life decisions for people | 9 | 16 | 74 |
| Know people's thoughts and behaviors | 9 | 16 | 75 |

1. **Introduction:** This bar chart deals with US adults' feelings about six possible AI tasks; figures in %, US adults, 2021 (survey).
2. **Main idea:** Generally speaking, people like AI for simple, practical tasks, but they are worried when AI gets close to personal decisions and private thoughts.
3. **Details:** most striking figure 75 % concerned (thoughts/behaviours) vs. 9 % excited; life decisions 74 %; household chores 57 % excited (most popular); workplace 46 vs. 26; medical diagnosis divided 40 vs. 35; customer service 47 % concerned; trend: the more personal, the more concern (excitement 57 % → 9 %); "equally" only 16–27 %.
4. **Conclusion:** All in all, people accept AI as a helper, not as a decision-maker. Link to statistic A (surveillance, lack of human connection, AI too powerful). Critique: only US adults, 2021 – before ChatGPT (released Nov. 2022).
