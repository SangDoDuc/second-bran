## Stichpunkte (Englisch, so auf der Seite)

**Stage 2: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)**

**1. Characteristics**
- strong form of AI: machine intelligence
- humanlike abilities: thinks, learns and makes decisions
- general: not only one task, but (almost) every task a human can do
- transfers knowledge from one area to another
- understands context, plans and solves new problems on its own
- level of a human, not better (better than humans = ASI, stage 3)
- still hypothetical: no real AGI yet, experts disagree when it will come

**2. Examples**
- no real example yet, only goals and fiction
- aim of big companies: OpenAI, Google DeepMind
- today: chatbots like ChatGPT can do many tasks, but are no true AGI (debated)
- fiction: JARVIS (Iron Man), Data (Star Trek), Samantha (film Her)

**3. Impact on society**
- \+ medicine and research faster: new drugs, cures for diseases
- \+ help with big problems, e.g. climate change
- \+ dangerous or boring jobs done by machines
- − many jobs lost, e.g. in offices and services
- − loss of control: AI makes its own decisions
- − power in the hands of a few big companies
- − misuse: fake news, cyber attacks, weapons
- − ethical questions: who is responsible? rights for machines?
- → rules needed: laws (e.g. EU AI Act) and international control

## Kurzversion zum Vortragen (3 Sätze)

AGI is the second stage of AI: a machine that can think, learn and make decisions like a human in almost every area. It does not exist yet, but big companies like OpenAI and Google DeepMind are working on it. It could help medicine and research a lot, but it could also cost many jobs and become hard to control, so we need clear rules.
